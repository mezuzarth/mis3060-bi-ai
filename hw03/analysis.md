Based on my data, executive changes tended to precede earnings announcements. 17 of  26 events extracted were filed before the nearest earnings release, 8 came afterwards, and only 1 (Walmart board appointment - November 2025) fell within the same week. When the five Item 5.02 filings that my pipeline couldn't tie to a specific person were removed (most likely compensation disclosures rather than actual leadership changes), the split was still 15 before, 5 after, and 1 same week, so the direction held firm. However, the pattern does vary by company. Walmart accounts for a large share of the "before" events because a single January 2026 filing announced five leadership moves, while Microsoft was the outlier with every event filed after its earnings release. I also noticed that most changes landed 10 to 40 days away from an earnings date, which suggests these firms deliberately keep leadership news separate from earnings week instead of bundling it with results.

All five companies had at least one Item 5.02 filing in the past 12 months.
